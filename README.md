# Brazilian E-Commerce Analysis (Olist Store)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes a real-world public dataset from Olist, one of the largest department stores in Brazilian marketplaces. The dataset contains over 100,000 orders between 2016 and 2018.

The analysis explores business growth, logistics efficiency, and customer behavior using Power BI dashboards and data-driven insights.

This project demonstrates practical skills in:
- Data Analysis
- Business Intelligence
- Data Visualization
- E-commerce Analytics
- Trend Analysis

## Business Problem
E-commerce companies need to understand:
- How fast the business is growing
- Which products generate the most revenue
- How efficient delivery operations are
- What affects customer satisfaction
- How customers make payments

This project uses transactional data to answer these questions and provide business recommendations.

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains e-commerce transactions from 2016–2018 across multiple regions in Brazil. Data includes orders, customers, payment, Geolacation, products, logistics and customer reviews.

## Key Insights
1. Rapid Business Growth= The business experienced significant growth:
- Orders increased from 272 in 2016 to over 52,000 in 2018
-Total revenue reached approximately $16M
- Profit reached approximately $2.4M
- Profit margin remained stable at around 14–15%

2. Revenue Drivers= Top revenue categories included:
- Health & Beauty
- Watches & Gifts
These categories generated the highest sales by 2018.

3. Revenue Patterns= Revenue is concentrated during weekdays:
- Weekdays account for approximately 77% of total revenue
- Weekends account for about 23%

4. Top Revenue Cities= The highest revenue cities include:
- Sao Paulo
- Rio de Janeiro
Sao Paulo generated more than double the revenue of Rio de Janeiro.

5. Logistics Improvements= Delivery performance improved significantly:
- Average delivery time reduced from 19 days in 2016
- To approximately 12 days in 2018
This shows improved operational efficiency.

6. Delivery Time vs Customer Satisfaction= Shipping speed strongly affects customer satisfaction:
- 5-star reviews averaged about 10 days delivery
- 1-star reviews averaged about 21 days delivery
Slow delivery is a major driver of negative reviews.

7. Logistics Bottlenecks= Some cities experienced extreme delivery delays.
For example:
- Capinzal do Norte recorded average delivery times above 150 days
These locations represent logistics bottlenecks.

8. Payment Behavior= Most customers prefer digital payments:
- Credit Card: ~75%
- Boleto: ~18%
Credit cards are the dominant payment method.

9. Customer Retention Problem: Most customers appear as one-time buyers.
This suggests limited customer retention and an opportunity to increase repeat purchases.

## Business Recommendations
1. Improve Customer Retention by introducing loyalty programs, discounts for repeat purchases, and Subscription services. This can increase customer lifetime value.
2. Improve delivery performance in Capinzal do Norte, Novo Brasil, and other high shipping days cities. Possible solutions includes; local warehouses, and courier partnerships.
3. Improve Product Quality Monitoring by focus on high-complaint categories such as Health & Beauty as this may reduce cancellations and negative reviews.
4. Encourage more customers to leave reviews through email reminders, discounts, reward points as more reviews improve customer insight.

## Future Outlook
If customer retention strategies and logistics efficiency continue to improve, Olist could significantly increase customer lifetime value and long-term revenue growth. Strengthening repeat purchase behavior and maintaining delivery reliability will likely be key drivers of sustainable expansion.
